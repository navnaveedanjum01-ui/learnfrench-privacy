1. Who we are

Learn French (“Learn French the Friendly Way”) is an Android app for learning French from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2), with vocabulary, phrases, grammar, stories, quizzes, games and spoken pronunciation. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. Learn French is developed and published by Lvenia Apps (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at techfriend1124@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

Your learning progress

Your XP, streak, completed lessons, quiz and game results, the topics you find difficult, saved words, notebook entries, review schedule and app settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Anonymous usage events, such as finishing a lesson, quiz or game and its score, are counted by Google Analytics for Firebase (see section 6); your saved words, notebook entries and review schedule are not.

Pronunciation audio

French words and sentences are spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (usually Google’s speech services), using an on-device French voice. The app keeps the generated audio in its own storage on your phone so it plays instantly next time. This audio is not sent to us.

Connection checks

Because the app needs the internet (see section 5), it checks your connection when it starts, when your network changes and when you return to the app. To do this it sends small requests to connectivitycheck.gstatic.com and www.google.com (Google), 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare), and Google’s ad servers googleads.g.doubleclick.net and pagead2.googlesyndication.com. These requests contain no personal information; like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to the server. The app also reads your phone’s Private DNS setting so it can tell you if it is blocking ads. That setting is used only on your phone and never sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone recordings, location (GPS) or messages, and we do not use crash-reporting services. Anonymous usage statistics are described in section 6.

Daily reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules up to three learning reminders a day (for example a word of the day, a short quiz and a streak reminder). They are created on your phone from your progress and nothing is sent to us to make them. You can change their times or turn each one off in Profile > Settings > Reminders, or turn off all notifications for the app in Android Settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Learn French is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland). The app may show:

  • a banner at the bottom of some lists and reading screens, and sponsored cards within some screens (such as Home, Learn, Practice and topic pages), all clearly labelled;
  • a full-screen ad between activities, for example before a topic, lesson, quiz, story or game opens, or after you finish one, at most once a minute;
  • a full-screen ad when you open the app, or when you return to it after a while;
  • optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch to earn bonus XP.

Whether ads are shown is controlled remotely, and they may be switched off at any time.

To show, measure and, where allowed, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (and, on newer Android versions, Android’s privacy-preserving ad features such as Topics and attribution reporting);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language, screen size and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information goes directly from the app to Google; we do not receive it. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Depending on your consent and location, Google may work with other ad partners listed in its consent message.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Learn French uses Google’s consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to ask for your consent before your data is used for personalized ads. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads, which use less data.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: open the Profile tab, tap Settings, then tap Ad privacy choices.

5. Internet connection and ad blockers

Learn French needs an internet connection. If your phone is offline, the app shows a message asking you to connect and try again.

Because the app is funded by ads, it cannot be used while ads are switched on but blocked, for example by a Private DNS ad blocker, an ad-blocking app or VPN, or a DNS filter. In that case the app explains how to turn the blocker off. Your progress is kept and is available again as soon as the problem is fixed.

6. Google Firebase: settings, usage statistics and messages

Learn French uses these Google Firebase services. They use a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the app, not linked to your name or account) and basic app and device information, such as the app version, Android version, device model, language and approximate country.

  • Remote Config downloads a few settings, such as whether ads are switched on and which ad units to use.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase records anonymous usage events: which screens are opened, when lessons, quizzes, reviews and games are finished (with scores), whether a notification was opened, your app language, a rough learner level and streak range, and how long and how often the app is used. We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. The app does not send your advertising ID to Analytics, and Analytics data is not used for ad personalization.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers occasional messages we send to all learners or to learners of one app language (for example new lessons or a weekend challenge). To do this, the app registers a messaging token and subscribes to general topics for its language.

Firebase does not receive your notebook, saved words or anything you type. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes. Information is shared with Google (and, for the connection check, Cloudflare) only as described in sections 2 to 6, by the services built into the app. Because we do not store your data on any server, we have no personal data of yours to hand over to anyone else.

9. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: your progress stays until you use Reset all progress (Profile > Settings) or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also removes the saved pronunciation audio.
  • With Google: Google keeps advertising and Firebase data for the periods described in its policies. Analytics usage events are kept for at most 14 months.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

10. Security

Your progress and saved words stay on your phone and are protected by Android’s app sandbox. The app’s connections to Google services and the connection checks use encrypted HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data the app handles to a minimum.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Where we rely on consent (personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you can withdraw it at any time with Ad privacy choices. Other uses, including anonymous usage statistics, rely on our legitimate interest in providing, improving and funding a free app. You can turn off reminders in Profile > Settings > Reminders, and all notifications from the app in Android Settings.

Because we do not hold your data, most requests are best made to Google, for example through My Ad Center or the tools in the Google Privacy Policy. You can also contact us (section 14), and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

Learn French is made for a general audience of French learners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, contact us and we will help.
