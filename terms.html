3. What Learn French does

Learn French ("Learn French the Friendly Way") is an Android app for learning French from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2). It includes vocabulary, phrases, grammar, stories, quizzes, games and spoken pronunciation, together with progress tracking such as XP, streaks and review scheduling.

All learning content is built into the App. Your progress, saved words, notebook and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. French words and sentences are spoken using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device.

The App is free and is supported by advertising from Google AdMob. It does not currently offer subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid features.
